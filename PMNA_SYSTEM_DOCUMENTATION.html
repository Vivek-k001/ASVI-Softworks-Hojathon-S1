1. Introduction & Hyperlocal Problem Statement

In Tier-2/3 commercial hubs like Perinthalmanna and Angadipuram, local retail businesses face significant digital marketing hurdles:

  • Generic Dilution: National aggregators (Swiggy, Amazon) cater strictly to standard food delivery or warehouse shipping, offering zero discovery for local footwear stores, textile boutiques, mobile accessory shops, or jewelry showrooms.
  • Social Media Noise: WhatsApp statuses and Instagram stories expire within 24 hours and fail to provide searchable, organized, or location-filtered deals.
  • Print Ad Costs: Traditional local newspaper commercial display ads (e.g. Malayala Manorama) cost thousands of rupees for a single morning print run that cannot provide interactive Google Maps directions, click-to-call, or live inventory updates.

The PMNA Solution: A unified digital platform where local shoppers browse verified daily deals and seasonal sales with zero generic filler, and local shops subscribe for a dedicated, high-impact front-page commercial showcase.

4. Shopkeeper Subscription & Monetization Model

PMNA generates recurring platform revenue through monthly showcase advertising subscriptions paid by local shopkeepers:

  • Configurable Pricing: The monthly showcase fee is set to ₹99/month by default and can be updated dynamically by the Admin through the Admin Control Panel.
  • Strict Public Visibility Filter: Only shops with an active subscription and unexpired date appear in the Home Screen "Daily Deals & Offers" showcase.
  • Graceful Degradation: If a subscription expires, the showcase is automatically hidden from public discovery without deleting the shop profile or offers.
  • Payment Gateway Flow: India-friendly Razorpay-compatible simulation supporting UPI QR Code scanner, UPI VPA (e.g. pmna.perks@upi), and Cards with server-side activation.
